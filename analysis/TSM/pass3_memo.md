# TSM — Taiwan Semiconductor — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
TSMC is the tollbooth of the entire AI economy — 66.2% gross margin, profit +58%, >30% guided 2026 growth, and a compulsory 2nm migration for every major AI designer in 2027 with wafer prices *rising*. The operational case is flawless; the investment case is not: after +91% in twelve months to $436.59 (~33x trailing), the historical Taiwan discount has evaporated at precisely the moment AI concentration (61% of revenue) peaked. Expected IV: $263.30.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic** — specifically, the market now prices TSM like a US megacap without the geopolitical tail. Our base case simply restores a 20x terminal multiple (a modest cross-strait discount) and lands ~40% below the quote. No operational miss is required for that reversion — sentiment alone suffices.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Regulatory/geopolitical (High):** cross-strait risk is unhedgeable and structural; its price has gone to zero while the risk hasn't.
2. **Concentration:** 61% HPC revenue makes TSM the largest single casualty of any AI-capex digestion.
3. **Margin dilution:** 2nm ramp depreciation and overseas-fab premiums (Arizona/Japan/Germany) against the 66% GM.

## 4. Key catalysts
- **Q2 2026 results, July 16, 2026** (guide $39–40.2B, GM 65.5–67.5%)
- 2nm risk production (2H 2026) → high-volume 2027 with rising wafer prices
