# MSFT — Microsoft Corporation — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
Microsoft is operationally excellent (revenue +18%, Azure +40%, AI run-rate $37B growing +123%) and emotionally broken (−30% from its high, worst month in 25 years) for one reason: ~$190B of calendar-2026 capex crushed quarterly FCF by 22% while ~45% (~$281B) of its $625B backlog rides on a single customer — OpenAI. At 22.8x trailing it is the cheapest megacap AI spender; our expected IV is $325.30 vs $388.84.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market roughly correct.** The drawdown already repriced the capex fear; the residual ~16% premium to our IV is the market granting more benefit of the doubt on the OpenAI-linked backlog than we do. This becomes a buy on either a further OpenAI-scare flush toward ~$325 or the first sequential FCF-margin improvement.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **OpenAI concentration (primary model risk):** ~$281B of contracted backlog on one counterparty with unproven economics — unprecedented at this scale.
2. **Capex peak timing:** if CY2027 capex exceeds CY2026's ~$190B, the FCF recovery slips and the multiple stays compressed.
3. **Copilot monetization:** the enterprise-adoption bridge between AI capex and AI profits is reportedly wobbling.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q4 FY2026 results (~late July): guide $86.7–87.8B, Azure +39–40% cc
- Sequential FCF-margin improvement (the explicit cycle-peak signal)
- Copilot enterprise seat metrics
