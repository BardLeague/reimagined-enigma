# SOUN — SoundHound AI — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
SoundHound is attempting to buy its way to scale: +52% organic growth to a record $44.2M quarter, now merging all-stock with LivePerson (also lossmaking) toward a $500M combined revenue target with 25 of the Fortune 100. The unresolved problem is economics, not demand: 31.1% gross margin and −$26.7M quarterly EBITDA mean profitability is unproven at every scale reached so far, while the ~$2.8B cap (~12x forward sales) already pays for the transformation.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** The market prices the $500M revenue story with software margins attached; the reported margin structure says compute-heavy AI services. Expected IV $3.45 vs $6.96 — and the stock is already 69% below its 52-week high, so this is the *discounted* version of the story.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Technological/competitive (High/High):** big-tech voice and agentic stacks can bundle at zero marginal price.
2. **Margin transformation (primary model risk):** if gross margin can't migrate from ~31% toward 60%+, no revenue level produces meaningful FCF.
3. **Bankruptcy/dilution (Medium):** $216M cash against ~$26.7M/quarter burn plus merger integration costs — about two years of runway.

## 4. Key catalysts
- LivePerson merger close (2H 2026) and combined guidance
- Quarterly gross-margin trajectory (the number that decides everything)
- OASYS agentic platform traction
- FY2026 guidance ($225–260M) delivery
