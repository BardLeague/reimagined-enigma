# VRT — Vertiv Holdings — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-09)

## 1. Thesis
Vertiv is the power-and-cooling arms dealer of the AI datacenter build-out at full throttle — 252% order growth, $15B backlog, +23% sales with expanding margins — and the stock has fully collected it (+145% in a year to $333, ~44x forward). Order books this hot are bullwhip territory: they overstate steady-state demand, and the multiple prices the boom as permanent. Expected IV: $229.50.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** The $15B backlog genuinely locks 2026–27 revenue; the disagreement is entirely about 2028+ order rates. Our base case converts the backlog in full and still lands ~31% below the quote because a terminal year cannot sustain boom-era ordering.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Bullwhip (primary model risk):** 252% order growth includes double-ordering that unwinds when lead times normalize.
2. **Competitive (High):** Schneider and Eaton bring bigger service networks and balance sheets to the liquid-cooling fight.
3. **Single-factor exposure:** hyperscaler capex — the same variable as the whole AI complex.

## 4. Key catalysts

- Liquid-cooling retrofit wave (AI rack densities)
- Hyperscaler capex announcements (AMZN $200B / MSFT ~$190B feed the funnel)
