# PLTR — Palantir Technologies — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-07)

## 1. Thesis
Palantir is executing close to flawlessly — Q1 2026 revenue +85% y/y to $1.633B, US commercial +133%, FY2026 guidance raised to $7.65–7.662B with $4.2–4.4B adjusted FCF. The investment question is purely price: at $133.36 and ~145x trailing earnings, the market pays today for hypergrowth lasting well past 2030.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
It mostly doesn't at this price. Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** The current price embeds ~45%+ compound revenue growth for five years at ~50% FCF margins and a 35x+ exit multiple; our base case has growth decelerating to the low-20s by 2030. The July rally (Nvidia partnership, Army NGC2, DA Davidson upgrade) re-opened the valuation gap that the early-2026 drawdown (-27–30%) had partially closed.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (High):** foundation-model vendors and hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Databricks) attacking AIP differentiation and pricing power.
2. **Regulatory/contract (Medium):** UK NHS contract (~$440M) faces parliamentary opposition to renewal; expires early 2027.
3. **Technological (Medium):** rapid model commoditization could compress the value of the orchestration layer.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q2 2026 earnings, early August 2026 (next guidance checkpoint)
- Nvidia sovereign-AI partnership deployments (announced July 2026)
- Army NGC2 program milestones (awarded July 2026)
