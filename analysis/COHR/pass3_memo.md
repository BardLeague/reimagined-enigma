# COHR — Coherent Corp. — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-07)

## 1. Thesis
Coherent is a core AI-optics franchise executing well — Q3 FY26 revenue $1.81B (+21% y/y), datacom 75% of revenue growing 41%, backlog visible to 2028, and NVIDIA validation via a $2B equity stake plus multiyear CPO supply agreement. But the company currently generates **negative** free cash flow (capex ~70% of operating cash flow), and at $327.59 the market is capitalizing cash flows that do not yet exist.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** At ~9x run-rate sales on a negative-FCF business, the price assumes sustained 20%+ growth AND rapid capex normalization simultaneously; our base case credits the growth but staggers the cash conversion, yielding $189 expected IV. The -9.5% week is profit-taking in crowded AI-optics positioning, not yet a valuation reset.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (High):** optics has repeatedly commoditized; Lumentum, InnoLight, and in-house hyperscaler programs all attack the margin pool.
2. **Technological (Medium):** CPO transition timing cuts both ways — slippage kills catalysts, acceleration cannibalizes pluggables.
3. **FCF conversion (primary model risk):** if capex stays elevated through the cycle, terminal FCF never materializes at modeled scale.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q4 FY2026 results, August 2026 (guide: $1.91–2.05B revenue, EPS $1.52–1.72)
- 1.6T transceiver ramp margin evidence
- NVIDIA CPO agreement milestones
- FCF inflection (the datapoint that would change the rating)

## 7. Thesis breakers (verbatim)
- Datacom revenue growth decelerating below 20% y/y for two consecutive quarters
- FCF still negative in FY2027 (capex normalization thesis fails)
- NVIDIA CPO agreement descoped, delayed, or dual-sourced away
- Non-GAAP gross margin falling below 36%
- A major hyperscaler moving transceivers in-house or to a competing supplier

## 8. Position size & probabilities
0% at current price; revisit near expected IV (~$189) or on positive FCF inflection with growth intact. Probability of success: 30%. Probability of permanent loss: 25%.
