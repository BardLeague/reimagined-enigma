# ADBE — Adobe Inc. — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-09)

## 1. Thesis
Adobe is priced for a decline that has not appeared in a single reported number: the stock fell 30–37% in 2026 on generative-AI disruption fear while revenue grew 12.7% (a beat), FY guidance was *raised* to $26.5–26.6B, ARR grew 10.2%, and the FCF yield reached 13.15%. At $221.54 (~9x forward earnings, ~10x FCF) the market and the filings describe two different companies. We believe the filings, with a haircut.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too pessimistic** — the first such verdict in 23 analyses. The mispricing mechanism is a narrative shock (Anthropic's Claude Design launch in April, plus Midjourney/Sora/Canva) hitting a mature-growth multiple all at once, compounded by a CEO transition. The price implies revenue decline within two years; reported ARR is still compounding at 10%.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Technological (High):** generative tools attack the prosumer low end first, then move upmarket — the classic disruption path, and it is genuinely underway.
2. **Value trap (Medium):** cheap software with a secular question is how Citrix and Teradata traded on the way down; ARR deceleration is the tell to watch.
3. **Data caveat (material):** one aggregator prints SBC as $1.94B *quarterly* (implausible vs Adobe's ~7%-of-revenue norm); we modeled it as annual and deducted it fully — but if it is truly quarterly, the value case fails. Verify against the 10-K before deploying capital.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q3 FY2026 results (~September 2026): guide $6.67–6.72B — a third beat-and-raise cracks the narrative
- Firefly / AI-influenced ARR disclosures (direct rebuttal evidence)
- Buybacks retiring ~10% of shares annually at these prices
- CEO transition resolution

## 7. Thesis breakers (verbatim)
- Ending ARR growth below 7% y/y (erosion accelerating)
- First y/y decline in Creative Cloud net new ARR
- FY2026 revenue guidance cut (reversing the raise)
- SBC verified above $4B/yr (per-share economics materially worse than modeled)
- Enterprise seat churn disclosures or a flagship enterprise loss to a generative-AI competitor

## 8. Position size & probabilities
3–4% starter with room to add. Probability of success: 60%. Probability of permanent loss: 15%.
