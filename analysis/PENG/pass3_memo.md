# PENG — Penguin Solutions — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
Penguin Solutions is the watchlist's small-cap sleeper: a $478.7M record quarter (+48% y/y, beating by 10%), AI businesses at 74% of sales growing 104%, guidance raised from +12% to +22% sales with EPS to ~$2.60, and a ~30% FY2027 growth frame — at ~26x FY26 EPS, the only sane growth multiple here outside NVIDIA. The drags are real but disclosed: gross margin −3.6pp on mix, Advanced Computing shrinking 15–20% on the Penguin Edge wind-down and Meta exit.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market roughly correct.** At ~20x the FY2027 frame the market modestly front-runs delivery; our base case haircuts for the Meta/Edge drag and lands ~19% below the quote. This is a watch-and-pounce name, not a mispricing today.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (High):** Dell, Supermicro, HPE and white-box vendors chase every non-hyperscale AI deal.
2. **Growth-mix transition (primary model risk):** if AI backlog converts slower than the legacy drag lands, the FY27 frame slips and the multiple compresses.
3. **Margin mix:** AI infrastructure is structurally lower-margin; GM already −3.6pp y/y.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q4 FY2026 results (~October 2026) against raised guidance
- FY2027 outlook formalization (~30% growth frame)
- Backlog conversion (demand currently outpacing reported sales)
- Analyst target catch-up (street targets below the market price)
