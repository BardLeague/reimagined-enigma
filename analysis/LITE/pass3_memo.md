# LITE — Lumentum Holdings — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-07)

## 1. Thesis
Lumentum is printing the best operating numbers in AI optics — Q3 FY26 revenue +90% y/y to $808.4M, operating margin up 2,140bps to 32.2%, sold out and under-shipping demand by ~30%, Q4 guided to ~$1B. The stock has fully found the story: at $728.32 (~$57B cap, ~61x annualized forward EPS), our bull case merely equals the current price, so the risk/reward is asymmetrically negative despite superb execution.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** The price assumes hypergrowth persists well past FY2028; optics cycles have historically ended abruptly when supply catches demand. Our base case (deceleration without collapse, ~$437 IV) still sits ~40% below the quote. The -7.05% week is the crowded AI-optics trade leaking, not a reset.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (High):** Coherent, InnoLight, and in-house hyperscaler optics target the same sockets once supply normalizes.
2. **Bankruptcy/leverage (Medium):** meaningful debt and tight current ratios — leverage into a cyclical peak; management already raised preferred stock and extended maturities.
3. **Cycle duration (primary model risk):** the whole bear-bull spread ($90–$746) is one variable — how long demand outruns supply.
