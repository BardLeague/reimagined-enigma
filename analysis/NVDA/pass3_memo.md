# NVDA — NVIDIA Corporation — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-07)

## 1. Thesis
NVIDIA is the only name on this watchlist where the AI story is already arithmetic: $81.6B quarterly revenue (+85% y/y), ~75% gross margin, a record $49B of quarterly free cash flow, and a $91B guide — while the stock sits flat for 2026 at 29.8x trailing earnings, down from its own 44x average. The multiple derated without the earnings miss; expected IV of $225.50 sits above the $192.53 price.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market roughly correct** — this is a fair-price-for-a-wide-moat situation, not a mispricing windfall. The +14.6% margin of safety exists because the market prices meaningful deceleration and a possible capex digestion year; our base case is modestly more constructive, backed by the networking franchise (+263% y/y) and the China option (zero in guidance).

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (Medium):** hyperscaler custom ASICs eroding compute share exactly where terminal value lives (years 3–5).
2. **Regulatory (Medium):** China export escalation; Taiwan supply-chain tail risk.
3. **Capex cycle (primary model risk):** AI infrastructure spend has never had a downcycle at this scale; a flat FY2028 makes the bear case ($98) central.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q2 FY2027 results, August 2026 (guide $91.0B ±2%)
- GB300/NVLink networking ramp (second franchise forming)
- Any China datacenter policy thaw (pure upside vs guidance)
- Ongoing ~50%-of-FCF capital returns ($20B in Q1 alone)
