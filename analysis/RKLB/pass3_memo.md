# RKLB — Rocket Lab Corporation — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-07)

## 1. Thesis
Rocket Lab is genuinely becoming the credible non-SpaceX space company — record Q1 2026 revenue of $200.3M (+63.5% y/y), 38.2% gross margin, $2.2B backlog, and its largest-ever contract for Neutron and Electron through 2029. But at $87.38 (~$60B market cap on ~$800M annualized revenue) with no demonstrated free cash flow, the security is priced for an outcome our valuation cannot underwrite.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
It is the inverse of an opportunity. Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic** — the price requires roughly 10x our base-case year-5 FCF, simultaneously capitalizing Neutron success, share gains, and SpaceX-class economics. The +24% week (contract momentum, sector rotation) widened the gap further.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (High):** SpaceX overhang on pricing and capital attention (incl. anticipated SpaceX IPO).
2. **Bankruptcy/funding (Medium):** pre-FCF business in its most capex-intensive year; dilution risk if markets close.
3. **Technological (Medium):** Neutron debut is binary execution risk into a stock priced for success.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Neutron debut launch (2026) — the de-risking event
- Q2 2026 earnings (August 2026)
- Continued US defense awards (e.g., $190M Anduril hypersonic program)
- Integration progress on Mynaric and Motiv acquisitions
