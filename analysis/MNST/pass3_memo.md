# Investment Committee Memo — MNST (Monster Beverage Corporation)

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Price:** $96.87 · **Verdict: AVOID**

## 1. Thesis

Monster is executing its strongest growth phase in a decade — Q1 2026 net sales +26.9% to $2.35B (first $2B+ quarter, an 8.9% beat) with international sales +44.9% now ~45% of revenue on Coca-Cola's distribution network. The business is wide-moat, net cash, and genuinely accelerating; the stock, however, has already re-rated +31.6% in 90 days to ~47x trailing earnings and trades above the FactSet mean target ($93.55). At a 10% hurdle rate the price cannot be underwritten.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists (or doesn't)

Expectations gap verdict: **market too optimistic**. The key variable is FCF conversion: at ~47x FCF, the price requires 20%+ FCF growth compounding for years, while FY2025 FCF was $1.97B and gross margin is compressing (55.0% vs 56.5% y/y). The P&L momentum is real; the cash-flow path implied by the price is steeper than the reported cash-flow trend.

## 3. Key risks

1. **Valuation** — price already exceeds the mean analyst target after the 90-day run; little margin for execution slip.
2. **Margin structure** — international mix is structurally dilutive (geographic mix, aluminum, freight); gross margin fell 150bps y/y.
3. **Regulatory** — energy-drink caffeine/youth-marketing restrictions are a persistent overhang unpriced at a premium multiple.

## 4. Key catalysts

- Q2 2026 earnings (~August 2026) — continuation of 25%+ international-led growth.
- Two-for-one stock split effective **2026-08-10**.
- Progress against management's ~$10B-by-FY2027 revenue glidepath.
- Buyback: ~$400M authorization remaining (~$100M repurchased in Q1 2026).
