# CRM — Salesforce — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-09)

## 1. Thesis
Salesforce is the second wide-moat software franchise 2026 has priced for agentic extinction while its filings print growth: EPS +52%, FCF $6.6B in Q1, Agentforce ARR $1.2B compounding at +205%, FY27 guided +10–11% — against a stock at a three-year low, −45% from highs, ~10x FCF, with a $25B buyback running. Expected IV: $194.00 vs $165.84.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too pessimistic.** The price pays for the bear case (agents retire seats, revenue stagnates) while reported results print the base case. Two legitimate stains keep it cheaper than ADBE on quality-adjusted terms: five acquisitions this year (each dropped the stock) and an Agentforce base still too small to carry the P&L.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Technological (High):** seat cannibalization — identical to ADBE's question, equally unresolved; NRR below 100% is the confirmation signal.
2. **Capital allocation (grade C):** the acquisition habit could burn the discount before the narrative resolves.
3. **Value trap (Medium):** cheap software with a secular question; the analyst floor is still descending.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q2 FY2027 results (~late August); H2 re-acceleration checkpoint
- Agentforce ARR trajectory
- $25B buyback execution at ~10x FCF
