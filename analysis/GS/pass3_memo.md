# GS — Goldman Sachs — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
Goldman is executing at the absolute top of its game — $17.23B net revenues (+14%), EPS $17.55, ROE 19.8%, record Global Banking & Markets revenue with advisory up 89% in an M&A boom. That is precisely the problem: at $1,026.29 (~19x trailing vs the ~10–12x banks historically command), the market is capitalizing cycle-peak earnings as permanent. Expected IV: $787.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** Our base case pays 12x modestly-normalized earnings and lands ~24% below the quote — and unusually, the sell side agrees (consensus Hold, average target ~$978, below the market). Advisory +89% quarters are historically followed by mean reversion, not encores.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Cycle normalization (primary model risk):** the historical norm after quarters like Q1 is a 25–35% earnings decline; our bear case is the base rate, not the tail.
2. **Value trap (Medium):** ~14.6x forward looks reasonable on peak E.
3. **Regulatory (Medium):** capital-requirement changes can throttle the buyback with little notice.

## 4. Key catalysts
- **Q2 2026 earnings, July 14, 2026** — the immediate cycle test
- M&A/IPO pipeline conversion data
- Capital-return announcements
- Asset & Wealth Management mix progress
