# AVGO — Broadcom Inc. — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
Broadcom is the custom-silicon toll road of AI: Q2 FY26 revenue $22.19B with record FCF, Q3 guided to $29.4B carrying $16B of AI semiconductor revenue (+200% y/y), FY26 AI revenue guided $56B and 2027 reiterated above $100B, with the Apple relationship now locked through 2031. At $373.90 (~62x trailing) the market pays roughly full value for that delivery — our expected IV is $334.80.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market roughly correct.** The price embeds near-full realization of the 2027 $100B+ AI target; we weight a modest shortfall. The disagreement is ~12% — fair-value territory, not a mispricing. The first hairline cracks (Q2 consensus miss, lighter Q3 AI guide, Erste downgrade July 7) explain why the stock pays you to wait for a capex-scare entry.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Custom-ASIC program risk (primary model risk):** a handful of opaque hyperscaler XPU programs carry the 2027 target; cancellations surface without warning.
2. **Competitive (Medium):** NVIDIA pricing/ecosystem retaliation against custom silicon.
3. **Regulatory (Medium):** China exposure and antitrust attention on the software bundle.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q3 FY2026 results (~September 2026): $29.4B guide, $16B AI revenue
- New XPU customer announcements beyond Google/Meta/Apple
- Apple 2031 extension follow-through (announced July 2026)
