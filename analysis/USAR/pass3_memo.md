# USAR — USA Rare Earth — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
USA Rare Earth is the purest domestic mine-to-magnet play: Stillwater making commercial magnets since March (600 t/yr target by year-end), Round Top wholly owned, Carester processing IP, $1.75B cash after a $1.5B PIPE, and a pending $2.8B Serra Verde acquisition. But Q1 revenue was $5.7M at a 1.9% gross margin with a $67M net loss — the ~$4.2B market cap (~180x annualized revenue) is a national-security premium, not a capitalization of cash flows.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic** — or more precisely, the market is pricing something (strategic value to Washington) that a 10%-discount DCF deliberately refuses to pay for. Even a generous production ramp yields $9.60 expected IV vs $18.59. The same-week price prints of +9.3% and −15.4% confirm a headline-driven tape.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Technological (High):** rare-earth processing at scale is the graveyard of Western ventures.
2. **Bankruptcy/dilution (Medium):** $67M quarterly net loss, accelerating capex, and a $2.8B acquisition equal to 2/3 of the company's own market cap.
3. **China price offensive:** Beijing can crash rare-earth prices to strangle Western entrants — and has before.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Stillwater ramp to 600 t/yr by end of 2026
- Serra Verde close and integration
- US policy actions (price floors, DoD offtakes, stockpiling)
- Carester facility ramp late 2026–2027
