# Investment Committee Memo — ELF (e.l.f. Beauty, Inc.)

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Price:** $73.49 · **Verdict: AVOID**

## 1. Thesis

e.l.f. delivered a genuinely strong fiscal 2026 — net sales +25% to $1.636B with a 35% Q4 beat, all five brands growing, and Rhode compounding at ~80% on ~$390M annualized sales. But the stock rebounded ~47% in June (+21% in the five days into our snapshot) into a guide that management itself set below consensus (FY2027 adj EPS $3.27–$3.32 vs ~$3.61), while GAAP earnings fell 77% and net debt swelled to ~$552M post-Rhode. The price now embeds a re-acceleration the company did not underwrite.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists (or doesn't)

Expectations gap verdict: **market too optimistic**. Key variable: FY2027+ earnings growth — the market is paying a momentum premium for growth above a consensus number management just guided below. The June rally was driven by sentiment catalysts (Q4 beat, $55M tariff refund, e.l.f. Hair launch), not raised earnings power.

## 3. Key risks

1. **Earnings quality** — FY2026 GAAP EPS $0.45 vs adjusted $3.13; the valuation depends almost entirely on whether the add-backs are one-time.
2. **Leverage** — total debt $841.7M (from $256.7M) against $289.7M cash after the Rhode acquisition.
3. **Fashion-cycle/competitive** — TikTok-native mass cosmetics have short cycles; core growth already guided to decelerate to +12–14% from +25%.

## 4. Key catalysts

- **2026-08-05**: Q1 FY2027 earnings — first test of the guide and of cash conversion.
- Rhode expansion into Mexico + 7 European markets (FY2027).
- e.l.f. Hair rollout beyond TikTok Shop/D2C/Target online.
- Potential further tariff relief after the $55M refund.
