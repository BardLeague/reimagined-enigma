# AMAT — Applied Materials — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
Applied Materials is executing a record cycle — Q2 FY26 revenue $7.91B (+11% y/y) at ~50% gross margin, FY26 raised to $31.3B revenue / $11.09 EPS, equipment business growing >30% in calendar 2026. But at $622.59 (~56x forward EPS) the market has re-rated a cyclical equipment maker to compounder multiples at what history says is the top of an equipment cycle. Expected IV: $252.30.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** The entire ~$370 gap between price and expected IV is one disagreement: whether calendar-2026's >30% growth is the new permanent trend (market) or a strong cycle year in a cyclical industry (us). The whole semicap group (KLAC 66x, LRCX 61x trailing) is priced for permanence simultaneously — which is itself the warning.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Regulatory (High):** 85% Asia-Pacific revenue; $253M export-controls settlement already paid with ongoing obligations; further China restrictions bite directly.
2. **Value trap (Medium):** raised estimates make the multiple look justified right up until the digestion year.
3. **Multiple regime (primary model risk):** if the cycle turns, the E and the multiple compress together.

- TSMC/Samsung capex confirmations of >30% CY2026 semicap growth
