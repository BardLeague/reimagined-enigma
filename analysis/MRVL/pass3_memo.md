# MRVL — Marvell Technology — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
Marvell is the market's designated "next Broadcom" — record revenue +28%, FY27 guided to ~40% growth, $1.5B of custom silicon revenue growing 20%+, exceptional AI bookings across optics, switching, and XPU. The problem is the label: at $232.79 (~79x trailing, ~2x our expected IV even after July 7's -8% index-unwind drop), the price assumes Broadcom-scale custom wins at Broadcom margins. History says the second source wins smaller programs at thinner margins. Expected IV: $126.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** The entire gap is one word — "jackpot." Our base case prices Marvell as a strong second source; the market prices it as the next AVGO. The +130% run into S&P 500 inclusion was partly mechanical, and the July 7 unwind showed how much momentum was in the price.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (High):** Broadcom takes the biggest custom programs first, with deeper hyperscaler relationships.
2. **Second-source economics (primary model risk):** if FY27's ~40% is the cycle peak rather than the ramp's start, even our base case is generous.
3. **No defensive segment:** AI-capex digestion hits optics, switching, and XPU simultaneously.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q2 FY2027 results (~late August 2026): guide $2.70B ± 5%
- New custom XPU design-win announcements
- 1.6T optics and 51.2T switch ramps
- FY2028 outlook updates (already raised once)
