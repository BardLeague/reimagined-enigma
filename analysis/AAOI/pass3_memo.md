# AAOI — Applied Optoelectronics — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-07)

## 1. Thesis
AAOI is a levered catch-up bet on 800G optics: record Q1 2026 revenue of $151.1M (+51% y/y), first volume 800G shipments to a large hyperscaler, and a planned 6.5x capacity ramp to 650K+ units/month by year-end. But it remains lossmaking at record revenue, gross margin (29–30%) is structurally below peers, and its history includes abrupt hyperscaler socket losses. At ~$121 the market prices durable FCF margins the company has never achieved.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic.** At 10x+ forward sales for a breakeven assembler, the price assumes the scarcity moment becomes a durable franchise. Our expected IV is $30.10 — roughly a quarter of the quote. The -6.3% week tracks the broader AI-optics unwind.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Competitive (High):** price-taker position; 29–30% GM vs 45%+ at Coherent/Lumentum leaves no cushion when supply normalizes.
2. **Customer concentration (primary model risk):** one dual-sourcing decision by the lead hyperscaler ends the story — as it has before.
3. **Bankruptcy/funding (Medium):** lossmaking through a 6.5x capacity expansion; dilution risk if the ramp outruns demand.

## 4. Key catalysts

- Capacity ramp milestones toward 650K units/month by year-end 2026
