# ASML — ASML Holding N.V. — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
ASML is the highest-quality business on this watchlist — a literal EUV monopoly with a EUR 38.8B backlog, record bookings, 53% gross margin, and a freshly raised EUR 36–40B 2026 guide. The problem is arithmetic, not quality: after a +121% year, $1,855 is ~57x annualized earnings for ~13% growth. Our expected IV is $1,172.50, and even our bull case ($1,833) fails to reach today's price.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market too optimistic** — specifically about the multiple, not the business. The price requires either a permanent re-rating (paying 40x+ for monopoly earnings indefinitely) or growth acceleration the guidance doesn't support. Our base case already honors the monopoly with a 30x terminal multiple and still lands ~40% below the quote.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Regulatory (High):** US legislation threatening the remaining China sales channel — structural and outside management control.
2. **Multiple regime (primary model risk):** the re-rating from ~30x to ~57x is the entire investment question; it can reverse on sentiment alone.
3. **Technological (Medium):** High-NA execution against 2027 delivery targets, with immersion sales already starting slow.

## 4. Key catalysts
- **Q2 2026 results, July 15, 2026** — first bookings visibility since disclosure was pulled; guide EUR 8.4–9.0B
- Further FY2026 guidance moves (currently EUR 36–40B)
- High-NA EUV milestones toward 2027 targets
- US China-export legislation resolution (either direction)
