# KO — The Coca-Cola Company — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-09)

## 1. Thesis
Coca-Cola is the best-executing staple in the market — organic +10%, EPS +18%, margins to 35%, raised guidance, Coke Zero +13% absorbing the GLP-1 shock, and a *covered* dividend (72% of $12.2B guided FCF) in its 64th consecutive year of increases. The issue is price, and specifically the discount rate: at $83.78 (~30x FCF, beta 0.35), KO cannot clear this framework's fixed 10% hurdle under any scenario. Expected IV: $51.10.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market roughly correct.** The market and we agree on the cash flows and disagree only on the required return — the market prices KO at ~6–7%, the framework demands 10%. This "avoid" means *KO cannot deliver 10%/yr from here*, not that anything is wrong with the business. It is the mirror image of PEP: PEP looked safe and wasn't (uncovered dividend); KO is safe and priced accordingly.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Hurdle-rate mismatch (primary model risk, disclosed):** the verdict is discount-rate-driven; at 8% the base case roughly doubles.
2. **GLP-1 (Medium regulatory/structural):** patent expiries and oral forms expand usage in 2026; full-sugar volumes are the exposed base.
3. **Growth normalization:** +10% organic is above trend; reversion to 4–6% compresses the premium multiple.

## 4. Key catalysts
- Q2 2026 results (~July 22) — fourth consecutive beat expected
- FY26 delivery on raised +8–9% EPS guidance
- Zero/protein portfolio momentum
- $5.2B buyback execution
