# NOW — ServiceNow — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-09)

## 1. Thesis
ServiceNow is the cleanest bet on the *governance layer* of enterprise AI agents — and simultaneously the clearest victim if agents shrink the human seats its pricing is built on. Guidance was raised through a geopolitical shock ($15.735–15.775B subscription), Now Assist $1M+ ACV customers are +130%, and Jensen Huang called it "the operating system for enterprise AI agents." At $107.44 (~7x forward subscription revenue after a crash-and-rebound year), expected IV is $94.80.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market roughly correct.** The April crash (−14% in a day) and the July rebound (+6–15% depending on source) are the market repricing the agentic coin-flip in real time; our IV sits ~12% below the price — a small disagreement. The July 22 Q2 print is the first real evidence on cannibalization vs expansion.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Technological (High):** agents reduce seats — the pricing foundation — and nobody knows the net effect yet.
2. **Competitive (High):** every platform vendor (Microsoft, Salesforce) claims the same agent-orchestration ground.
3. **SBC:** cash economics are ~40% worse than headline FCF after adjustment.

## 4. Key catalysts
- **Q2 2026 results, July 22, 2026** — the agentic referendum
- AI Control Tower / Now Assist ACV metrics
- Nvidia partnership deepening
- Delivery against raised FY26 guidance
