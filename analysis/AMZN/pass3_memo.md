# AMZN — Amazon.com — Investment Committee Memo (2026-07-08)

## 1. Thesis
Amazon pairs the strongest operating quarter in years — $181.5B revenue (+17%), AWS reaccelerating to +28% on a $150B run-rate, above-consensus Q2 guidance — with the largest corporate capex commitment ever made ($200B for 2026), deliberately sacrificing near-term FCF to build AI infrastructure. At $250.56 (31x trailing) the market pays for successful conversion; our expected IV is $213.

## 2. Why this opportunity exists
Expectations-gap verdict: **market roughly correct.** We agree with the direction (conversion succeeds) and discount only the timing of the FCF ramp. The ~15% premium to expected IV is the price of AWS's reacceleration being publicly visible — the +6.5% week already collected it.

## 3. Key risks (top 3)
1. **Capex conversion (primary model risk):** if AI demand disappoints, $200B becomes stranded depreciation; the FCF trough deepens and lengthens.
2. **Competitive (Medium):** Microsoft and Google spending comparably — trench warfare compresses all AI-cloud returns.
3. **Regulatory (Medium):** persistent antitrust attention across retail and cloud.
